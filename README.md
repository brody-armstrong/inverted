# Inverted

Research-engineer-level football analytics for clubs with smaller analytics departments: player ratings and profiles, roster modelling and league research, built the way top clubs' research teams work. Questions are stated in advance, predictions are preregistered, every method is checked against outside evidence, and results that go against the hypothesis are reported as they are.

**Author:** Brody Armstrong

## Projects

| Folder | What it covers | Status |
|---|---|---|
| [`mls/`](mls/) | Major League Soccer: why the league is so unpredictable (Paper 1), why its clubs stay so close in quality (Paper 2), the Roster Investment Simulator, player profiles, the USL → MLS pathway and Designated Players | Papers 1–2 complete; simulator v1.0 live; Paper 3 planned |

Each project folder is self-contained: its own README, code, data dictionary, results and `requirements.txt`. Run its scripts from inside that folder.

## Data

No raw, third-party or private data is committed. Each project's `data/README.md` lists its sources, how the scripts fetch them and what is not redistributed.
