How it's built. Player impact comes from __MATCHES__ MLS matches (__SEASONS__): how closing betting lines moved with who was on the field, projected forward with age, salary and recent form. Team strength adds up each player's impact by expected minutes; seasons are simulated match by match.
How well it works. Backtested 2016–2026 from preseason rosters: average rank correlation with final points __BT_MODEL__ (last season's points: __BT_NAIVE__). Against preseason MLS Cup futures it reproduces the market's ranking (2025: __F25__, 2026: __F26__) with similar accuracy against final points. Typical error is about __RMSE__ points per club, half of it match luck.
Limits. Values every minute alike; outside-cause absences suggest elite players matter somewhat more than this, so star upgrades may be undervalued. Budget means guaranteed compensation: salary cap, DP, TAM and GAM mechanics are not modelled. Rosters come from the MLS Players Association salary release of __ROSTER_DATE__ (players under contract on that date); signings, departures and option decisions since then are missing and can be entered as scenarios. Signings are valued from past arrivals at a similar salary and age; transfer fees and previous leagues are not used yet.
