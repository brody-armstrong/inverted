# Roster Investment Simulator — roadmap

Paper 2 is frozen as v1.0 (complete 2026-11-12): results, executive summary, article and preregistered predictions do not change. The simulator is separate, living software; its updates never change the paper.

## Versions

| Version | When | Roster baseline | Framing |
|---|---|---|---|
| v1.0 (live) | Nov 2026 | Salary list updated by club Mar–Sep 2026 (642 of 793 matched) | Roster evaluation tool, not a 2027 forecast; offseason moves are user scenarios |
| v1.x | Winter 2026–27 | Updated club by club as option decisions, departures, trades and signings are announced | Each update can double as a case study ("before/after signing X") |
| 2027 preseason edition | Spring 2027, once clubs are roster compliant | Full refresh | The version compared against preseason futures and, later, final standings |
| Later editions | Summer window, playoffs | — | Summer transfer edition; playoff edition |

Open question before v1.x: whether to rebase on the official final 2026 roster (MLS is understood to freeze rosters in early October through MLS Cup) rather than the salary list. The rule and its source have not been verified yet.

## Feature ideas (not started)

1. **Option scenarios:** exercise/decline club options as one-click scenarios.
2. **Trade scenarios:** sell player A, receive player B: projected points, replacement value, roster weakness.
3. **Injury/suspension calculator:** with vs without a player; links directly to the absence study.
4. **Live playoff probabilities:** after each playoff match, update advancement and MLS Cup odds and projected opponents.
5. **Expansion-draft scenarios:** e.g. a club loses its best player, a second centre-back and a backup striker.
6. **Free-agent valuation:** report how past arrivals at this age and salary performed, not a single dollar value (comparables already in v1.0).

Constraint carried from the paper: the model is calibrated against the market and explains where disagreement occurs; it is never described as beating the market.
